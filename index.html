Personal Drive & Mail Access

This is a private tool used by one person, Jialin Li, to reach their own Google Drive files and email from their own Linux computer with open-source command-line programs (rclone and himalaya).

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not collect, sell or share anyone's data.
